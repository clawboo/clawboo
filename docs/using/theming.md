The dashboard comes in a light and a dark look. Pick one, or let Clawboo follow your computer's light or dark setting.

## Change the theme

<Steps>

<Step title="Find the switch">

In the account bar along the bottom of the left sidebar, beside your name and the **Settings** gear, is a three-part switch with icons only: a monitor for **System**, a sun for **Light** and a moon for **Dark**. Hover a part to see its name.

</Step>

<Step title="Click the one you want">

The whole dashboard changes at once, with no reload. The part you chose looks raised.

</Step>

</Steps>

| Choice     | What you get                                                                                                                                                             |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **System** | Follows your computer's light or dark setting, and changes as soon as that setting does. Hover it to see which look it is using now: "System (light)" or "System (dark)" |
| **Light**  | Always light                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Dark**   | Always dark                                                                                                                                                              |

## Good to know

- Clawboo starts in **Light** until you choose.
- Your choice is saved in this browser, for this address. Another browser, a private window, cleared site data, or the dashboard at another address, port or path prefix starts in **Light** until you choose there.
- One exception after an upgrade: a theme an earlier release saved for this address goes to the first dashboard under a path prefix that opens here, and that dashboard starts in the saved theme rather than **Light**.
- The welcome screen and the onboarding steps keep their bright sky whatever you choose.

## If you can't see the switch

- **The sidebar is hidden.** Press `Cmd+\` (macOS) or `Ctrl+\` (Windows, Linux), or click **Expand sidebar** at the start of the page's header. Hovering that button shows the sidebar, switch included, without docking it.
- **You are on Boo Zero's page.** The sidebar is only the team rail there, so the account bar stands upright and the switch's three parts are stacked.
