There is now an **Inbox** in the sidebar: what needs you and what changed since you last looked, with a badge that counts both. **Unread** lists tasks someone else changed or commented on since you last opened them; **Needs you**, **Mine** and **All** sit beside it. What you do yourself does not make a task unread for you, and an agent's progress does. Read state is kept on the server, per task, so it is the same in every browser and survives a restart. Behind a sign-in proxy each person has their own. The badge updates from the app's event stream, with no polling. An app installed on a phone gets nothing while it is closed, and catches up when you open it. The Board's "Since you last looked" chip stays in the browser as before, and a task you read in another browser stops counting in it.
