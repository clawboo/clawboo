The board now says what every waiting task waits for, and a person can be part of the work. An agent that blocks a task must give a reason, and Needs you shows it. A task waiting on another task or on its own sub-tasks stays out of Needs you and goes back to the queue when that work finishes. Tasks can be assigned to a person: they never start on their own, no agent can take them, and a Mine filter finds them. An agent can ask a person a question (a choice, a yes or no, or a list of tasks to accept or edit), which parks the task until the answer starts it again, or hand its task back to the person who asked for it. A person can reopen a finished task. On a local install, "a person" is one built-in person, and the check that a write came from the dashboard is a speed bump, not a boundary. See the security page.
