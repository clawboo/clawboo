Home is now a working page instead of a placeholder. It is the first row in the sidebar's nav, and its picker works like the Board's: **All teams**, then each team, starting on the team the sidebar shows; clicking a team in the team rail opens Home on it. A team's Home shows, top to bottom, its status line with who wrote it and when, what needs you (tasks waiting on you, approvals, goal updates an agent drafted, KPIs an agent suggested), its goals with health and progress, its KPIs, the requests in flight, spend against its budget, upcoming routines, pinned pages and the files its agents made. Sections with nothing in them are left out, and a new team gets prompts to add its first goal, KPI and page. **All teams** shows totals, one Needs you list for every team and a card per team. Every number says where it came from: computed from the board, reported by an agent (named, with its run), or typed by a person.

Each goal has its own page: its description, owner, target date, health and its history, progress over its linked tasks (each opens in the Board's drawer), its KPIs, the drafts to publish, edit or discard, published updates, cost (labeled when it includes estimates), child goals and pages. Done and dropped goals fold under the goal list and can be reopened. KPIs are added and edited on Home, with typed values, value history and suggested KPIs to accept or dismiss. Pages have a markdown editor and a history of every revision, with who wrote it and a way to restore an old one. A team's Home settings turn on weekly goal updates (a team routine that asks the lead for drafts) and, off by default because it costs model calls, a status line the lead writes.

Board cards and team chat task cards show the goal a task counts toward, its own or one it inherits from its request. The task drawer, **New task** and a request sent from team chat can pick a goal, and the Board's filter can show one goal's work, or only work toward no goal.
