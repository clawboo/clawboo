'clawboo': minor

Boo Zero can now hand work to a team from its own chat. Start a message with **@Team**, or say which team in plain words, and each hand-off becomes a task on that team's board, with its card in the chat and in the team's chat. When the task ends, the result comes back to Boo Zero's chat, even if Clawboo restarted in between, and Boo Zero tells you what came of it. With no team named, nothing starts and the chat asks which team, and a name that could mean two teams or teammates asks too. Your message is a request, with its own main task on each team's board, and it closes after Boo Zero's reply in this chat. On a team that asks first, several hand-offs in one reply wait for your answer. A hand-off also refuses plainly when the team or teammate is not there or is paused, or while Clawboo is stopping or updating, and a result under a pause waits and goes out on the resume. A hand-off shown in code or a quote starts nothing, so Boo Zero can show you a draft, and while reading a result it can go on only in that result's team. After you press Stop in this chat, a result that arrives starts no reply until your next message. This works on Clawboo Native, Claude Code, Codex and Hermes. An OpenClaw Boo Zero still cannot hand off from its chat, because its reply goes from your browser straight to the Gateway and the server never sees it, and it says so.
