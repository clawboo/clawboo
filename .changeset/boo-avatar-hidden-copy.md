An agent's Boo no longer loses its body when another copy of it is mounted but hidden. With the sidebar hidden, its agent grid stays in the page for the peek panel, and a Codex Boo elsewhere (a card on the Board, for one) took its colours and outline from that hidden copy and drew only its eyes and paws. Every Boo now draws from its own copy, Codex, Claude Code and Hermes Boos and the generated mascot alike, wherever the others are and whether or not they are shown.
