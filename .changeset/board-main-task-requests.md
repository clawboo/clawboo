Your request is now one card on the board. When a team's lead hands out work for something you asked in chat, your message becomes a main task, with every hand-off and plan step nested under it as a sub-task, on the board and in the chat. The card shows how far the work has got. It waits for its sub-tasks and moves to Done after the lead's summary, which also goes to chat. A plain answer still makes nothing. A sub-task that needs you stays in Needs you, labelled with its request. To add to a request, comment on its main task; a new message in the chat is a new request. Cancelling a main task, or marking it Done, cancels the sub-tasks still open and stops their runs. Helpers keep every level of hand-offs they had, and a main task never runs by itself. Tasks from before this stay as they are.
