Removing a route on the Org chart works without an OpenClaw Gateway, like adding one already did. Without a Gateway, **Remove Connection** on a route used to do nothing. If the agent's AGENTS.md cannot be read, the route now stays and **Failed to remove routing** appears, where it used to vanish from the canvas and come back later. The empty Org chart now says to create a Boo or deploy a team instead of "Connect to a Gateway to see your fleet."
