The Kanban board's lanes run unbroken to the bottom again. On a board with few cards, a faint line crossed every lane where its cards ended, left over from the rule that separates days in the Today, Week and Month views; those views keep it. Dropdown menus now fit their longest option, so the board's team picker reads "Growth" in full instead of "Gro..." when that team is checked, and the same holds for every other dropdown that was as narrow as its button, such as the Marketplace's sort.
