**Modifications**: this pack is adapted, not verbatim. Of fifty upstream skill
directories, the search-engine, paid-advertising, social-channel, image, video,
content-strategy and copywriting units were dropped because the Clawboo catalog
already ships agents for those cells, and the helper scripts, evaluation files
and reference trees were left behind entirely. The remaining units were merged
and re-scoped into twenty-six agents and rewritten from scratch into the Clawboo
SOUL, IDENTITY and TOOLS document set. Frontmatter was replaced with structured
catalog fields, every description was newly written, upstream tool integration
tables and vendor links were removed, cross-references to upstream skill names
were replaced with Clawboo agent names, and the long source files were cut down
editorially. The five teams, their rosters, the leader choices, the workflow
narratives and every routing edge are original Clawboo work; the upstream has no
team concept. Skill declarations, categories, tags, colours and emoji are
Clawboo's.
