**Modifications**: this pack is adapted, not verbatim. The upstream ships four
skill documents, and the large majority of their content is product plumbing:
transport and mode detection, authentication and API-key handling, installation
commands, command-line and tool-call surfaces, endpoint versions, file
conventions written to a workspace, and troubleshooting for a specific service.
All of that was dropped rather than reworded. No vendor name, product name,
endpoint, command, environment variable, tool name or documentation link from
the upstream appears anywhere in this pack, and no upstream shell script ships
here. The avatar-identity unit was not carried across at all, because its
substance is a file-and-identifier convention for one product. What was kept is
the production craft that transfers to any tool: conversational discovery
instead of a questionnaire, one idea per video, script structures by video type,
writing for the ear, front-loading the hook, pulling exact on-screen text into
its own list, deciding aspect ratio before composition, checking caption safe
areas per destination, and reviewing translated text before the final render.
The consent boundary on likeness and voice is Clawboo's addition. The body was
written from scratch into the Clawboo SOUL, IDENTITY and TOOLS document set,
frontmatter was replaced with structured catalog fields, and the description was
newly written. This pack ships no teams, because one agent is not a team. Skill
declarations, categories, tags, colours and emoji are Clawboo's.
