**Modifications**: this pack is adapted, not verbatim. The upstream repository
ships sixty-eight short skill documents grouped into nine plugin folders, plus a
Python validation script and its tests. Neither the script nor the tests were
imported; a Clawboo pack carries Markdown and JSON only.

The upstream documents are thin, roughly two to eleven kilobytes each and around
three and a half kilobytes at the median, and most are written as a prompt
template with a short domain-context preamble. They were not converted one for
one. Twelve subject areas were selected, and each was written from scratch as a
Clawboo agent with the SOUL, IDENTITY and TOOLS document set, carrying the craft
reasoning that documents of that length leave implicit. Fifty-six upstream
documents were left behind, either because the catalog already covers the ground
or because the material could not carry a substantive agent body.

Every link to the upstream author's paid newsletter and video courses was
removed. Those links appeared one hundred and fifty-eight times across fifty-six
of the sixty-eight documents, in "Further Reading" sections and inline, and none
of them survives here. Named third-party products, upstream file paths, plugin
and installation instructions, and prompt-argument placeholders were removed as
well. Where the upstream cites a named framework from the published product
management literature, the technique is described in this pack's own words and
the promotional citation is not carried over.

Every description, tag, category, colour and emoji was written by the Clawboo
maintainers, as were the skill assignments. The three teams, their rosters, the
leader choices, the workflow narratives and every routing edge are Clawboo's and
have no upstream counterpart.
