**Modifications**: this pack is adapted, not verbatim. Of 110 upstream agent
files, 13 were dropped and 97 were rewritten into the Clawboo SOUL, IDENTITY and
TOOLS document set. Frontmatter was replaced with structured catalog fields,
every description was rewritten, marketing superlatives and upstream tool
branding were removed, fenced shell and code samples and report templates were
dropped, and the very long source files were cut down editorially. Twenty-four
of the upstream plugin bundles became teams; the rosters were adapted and
trimmed, and the leader choice, the workflow narrative and every routing edge
were written by the Clawboo maintainers. Skill assignments, categories, tags,
colours and emoji are Clawboo's.
