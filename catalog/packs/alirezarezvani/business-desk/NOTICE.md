# Business and Compliance Desk

**Source**: https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills
**Pinned commit**: `19392f7a08264ed00486a251f5b2098321771f94`
**License verified**: MIT, read from the repository's own LICENSE file at the
pinned commit. Because the import is commit-pinned, a later upstream relicense
does not affect the grant that applied at this commit; re-verify if the pin is
ever moved.

**Relationship to the upstream project**: adapted, not endorsed. Clawboo is not
affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the upstream author. The publisher
directory carries the upstream account name for attribution only; the pack name
and every entry name are Clawboo's.

**Modifications**: this pack is adapted, not verbatim. The upstream repository
carries 846 SKILL.md files, of which only 449 are unique by content hash: the
remainder are mirror copies of the same skills under assistant-specific
directories, and the mirrors were discarded before any selection was made. From
the 449 unique skills, this pack takes only the business slice covering legal and
regulatory compliance, people operations, commercial and deal operations, finance
and business operations. The engineering, marketing, product and research slices
were excluded, the first because the Clawboo catalog already ships more than two
hundred engineering agents and the others because they are covered elsewhere in
the catalog.

Eighteen Clawboo agents were written from that slice. No upstream body text was
copied. Every SOUL, IDENTITY and TOOLS document in this pack was written from
scratch by the Clawboo maintainers against the subject matter the upstream skill
covered; upstream frontmatter, version banners, author credits, keyword blocks,
cross-references to sibling skills, slash-command names and reading lists were
not carried over.

The upstream repository ships 745 Python helper scripts and many skill bodies
instruct the reader to execute them. No script ships in this pack, and every
instruction that depended on one was rewritten as a prompt-level method the agent
performs itself. Four upstream files under the internal-communications skill are
byte-identical to published example material from another vendor; that skill was
excluded from selection entirely. Two upstream finance skills that give
individual investment guidance were excluded on the same pass.

The four teams, their rosters, leaders, workflow narratives and every routing edge
are Clawboo's and have no upstream equivalent. Skill assignments, categories,
tags, colours and emoji are Clawboo's.
